RentShield is operated by the developer identified on the Google Play listing. For privacy questions or requests, contact: YOUR_EMAIL@example.com (replace this placeholder before publishing).

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may process:

  • Phone number — used to send a one-time passcode (OTP) for sign-in and to associate your account with your profile and documents in our systems.
  • Profile information — such as your name, employer, and occupation when you choose to provide them in the App.
  • Photos and videos — content you capture or select for rental agreements and move-in condition documentation, stored so you can retrieve and use that record.
  • Approximate or precise location — when you allow it, we may attach coordinates and accuracy information to move-in evidence to document where media was captured.
  • Agreement-related text or images you submit — if you use agreement review features, that content may be sent to an automated review service as described below.
  • Technical data — standard data involved in operating the App over the internet (for example, IP addresses and timestamps processed by our service providers when you make requests).

3. How we use your information

  • To authenticate you and secure your account (OTP).
  • To store and display your profile and rental-related documentation you choose to save.
  • To provide address and place search when you use location search in the App.
  • To generate summaries or suggestions when you use agreement review features (if enabled in your build).
  • To comply with law, enforce our terms, and protect users.

4. Where data is processed (service providers)

We use third-party infrastructure that processes data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — database and file storage for profile and move-in evidence data. See Supabase’s privacy policy.
  • Our authentication API — a backend service (for example, hosted on Render) that sends and verifies OTPs. SMS delivery may be handled by Twilio when configured for production; see Twilio’s privacy policy.
  • Mapbox — processes search queries when you use location search. See Mapbox’s privacy policy.
  • Anthropic (Claude) — may process agreement-related content you submit for automated review features. See Anthropic’s privacy policy.
  • Google — when you install from Google Play, Google processes information according to Google’s policies.

We do not sell your personal information.
